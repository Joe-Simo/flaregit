Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and sign-in identifiers, handled by our authentication provider.
  • Your content: repositories, commits and task descriptions you submit, plus verification results and build output.
  • Usage data: run counts, plan status and operational logs needed to run and protect the service.

How we use it

To provide the service, enforce usage limits, prevent abuse, process payments and support you. We do not sell your data and we do not use your code to train AI models.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare — hosting, storage, compute and AI inference.
  • Clerk — sign-in and account management.
  • Polar — subscription billing and payments. We never see your card details.

Code excerpts sent to AI models are used only to generate the requested result.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Email support@flaregit.com to request deletion of your account and repositories.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each customer's repositories and state are isolated, and code runs in sandboxed containers without access to our secrets.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your data. Contact support@flaregit.com.

Questions: support@flaregit.com
